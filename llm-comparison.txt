My model is slightly more complex than the ChatGPT-generated model, but it is more complete for attendance analytics and cheating prevention. Both versions separate student and instructor profiles, represent enrollment with an explicit join table, and connect courses, lectures, and uploads with foreign keys. The human-designed version additionally protects courses from accidental instructor deletion, gives lectures unguessable unique QR identifiers, records the QR value decoded from each upload, prevents duplicate submissions by one student for the same lecture, and can store an image digest to detect a reused photo. The robot model is therefore shorter and easier to implement, while my model better captures the application requirements and enforces important rules in the database rather than relying only on future view code.
